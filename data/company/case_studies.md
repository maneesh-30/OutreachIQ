# Acme Analytics — Case Studies

## Lumen (project management SaaS, 180 employees)
**Problem:** PMs waited days for the data team to pull funnel reports; onboarding
drop-off was invisible.
**Solution:** Adopted Acme product analytics + session replay.
**Result:** Cut onboarding drop-off by 34% in one quarter. Reduced ad-hoc data
requests to the data team by 60%.

## Fathom Commerce (e-commerce platform, 320 employees)
**Problem:** Could not tell which checkout steps caused cart abandonment.
**Solution:** Funnel analysis + experimentation on checkout flow.
**Result:** A/B tested a one-page checkout; conversion up 18%, $2.1M incremental
annual revenue.

## Northwind Health (digital health app, 90 employees)
**Problem:** Needed analytics but had strict HIPAA/PII constraints.
**Solution:** Acme privacy-first mode with PII masking + EU data residency.
**Result:** Launched analytics in 3 weeks while staying compliant; retention
reporting now drives their roadmap.

## Common themes
- Teams without a dedicated data team get answers in hours, not days.
- Measurable lift in conversion/retention within one quarter.
- Privacy controls unblock regulated industries.
