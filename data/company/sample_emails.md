# Acme Analytics — Winning Outreach Emails (voice/style reference)

These are past cold emails that got replies. Use them as the TONE and STRUCTURE
reference: short, specific, one clear hook, one soft CTA. No corporate fluff.

## Example 1 (to a Head of Product at a PLG SaaS)
Subject: onboarding drop-off at {{company}}?

Hi {{first_name}},

Saw {{company}} just opened up self-serve signup — nice move. The usual pain
right after that is not knowing *where* new users stall before activating.

Lumen had the same gap; they cut onboarding drop-off 34% in a quarter after
seeing it in session replay. Happy to show you the three reports they used.

Worth a 15-min look next week?

— {{sender}}

## Example 2 (to a Head of Growth at an e-commerce platform)
Subject: the checkout step costing you conversions

Hi {{first_name}},

Most {{industry}} teams can see *that* carts get abandoned, but not *which*
step does it. Fathom Commerce found theirs, tested a fix, and lifted checkout
conversion 18%.

Takes about an hour to set up. Want me to send the funnel template they used?

— {{sender}}

## Style rules
- 3 short paragraphs max.
- One real proof point (a case study), named.
- End with a low-friction question, not "let me know your availability."
- Never more than one link. Never attach anything in the first email.
