# Acme Analytics — Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

## Firmographics
- **Industry:** B2B SaaS, e-commerce, digital health, fintech (any product-led company).
- **Size:** 50–500 employees.
- **Stage:** Series A to Series C; has a live web or mobile product with real usage.
- **Geo:** North America and EU (EU data residency is a selling point).

## Must-have signals (good fit)
- Has a product team or growth team.
- Product-led growth motion (self-serve signup, free trial, or freemium).
- Currently using spreadsheets, or a heavy tool (e.g. a raw warehouse + BI) that
  non-technical teams can't self-serve.
- Hiring for PM, growth, or product analyst roles (signals investment in product).

## Anti-signals (poor fit)
- Pure services/agency with no software product.
- Enterprise >2000 employees with an entrenched in-house analytics stack.
- Pre-product startups with no users yet.
- Hard offline/physical business with no digital product.

## Decision makers
- **Champion:** Head of Product, Head of Growth, Senior PM.
- **Economic buyer:** VP Product or VP Engineering.
