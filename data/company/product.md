# Acme Analytics — Product Overview

Acme Analytics is a B2B SaaS platform that gives product and growth teams
real-time insight into how users move through their web and mobile apps.

## What we sell
- **Product analytics** — funnels, retention, path analysis, cohort reports.
- **Session replay** — watch real user sessions to find where they drop off.
- **Experimentation** — built-in A/B testing tied directly to analytics events.
- **Alerts** — anomaly detection on any metric, pushed to Slack or email.

## Core value props
1. **Setup in under an hour** — one SDK snippet, auto-captures events; no data team required.
2. **Self-serve for non-technical teams** — PMs and marketers build reports without SQL.
3. **Privacy-first** — EU data residency, GDPR/CCPA compliant, PII masking by default.
4. **Transparent pricing** — flat per-seat, unlimited events; no surprise usage bills.

## Who it's for
Mid-market SaaS companies (50–500 employees) with a web or mobile product and a
product/growth team that needs answers faster than their data team can deliver them.

## Integrations
Segment, Snowflake, BigQuery, Slack, HubSpot, Salesforce, Webhooks, REST API.
